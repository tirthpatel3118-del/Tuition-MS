4.1 The Institute is responsible for deciding what Institute Data to enter, ensuring it is accurate and relevant, and using it only for lawful educational and administrative purposes.

## 5. Platform services and limitations

5.1 Depending on the subscription, permissions and features enabled, the Platform may support Institute profile and workspace administration, courses, batches, admissions, student profiles, fee balances, installments, direct fee collection, payment allocations, receipts, reminders, attendance and related reports.

5.2 Features may change, be improved, limited or retired. A feature is available only where it is enabled for the relevant account and environment. No feature description overrides the live product interface or a separately agreed order.

5.3 Messaging: current WhatsApp reminders are user-initiated/manual workflows that open WhatsApp or prepare a message for the user. Automated WhatsApp Business Cloud API delivery, automatic email delivery and SMS delivery are not promised by these Terms. The user must verify the recipient and message before sending. WhatsApp processes information under its own terms when the user opens or sends a message.

5.4 Attendance and biometrics: attendance functionality is available only to the extent enabled in the Platform and is entered or managed by authorised Institute users. Face recognition or other biometric attendance is not represented as a current feature. A future biometric feature would require separate product, privacy, security and legal review, appropriate notices and lawful permissions, and a suitable alternative where required.

5.5 The Platform is not an emergency service, student safeguarding service, legal-compliance certification, accounting opinion or replacement for an Institute's own statutory records.
