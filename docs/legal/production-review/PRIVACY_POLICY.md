## 1. Who this Policy applies to

This Policy explains how Tirth Patel, operating ClassBandhu as an individual for the purposes of this draft (ClassBandhu, we, us or our), handles personal data in connection with ClassBandhu Tuition, including:
- the public website, website enquiries and access requests;
- user accounts, authentication, invitations and onboarding;
- tuition/institute ERP workspaces;
- subscriptions, billing, support, security and platform administration.

This Policy covers ClassBandhu Tuition only. A separate product such as ClassBandhu School is not covered unless a separate notice is approved and published.

ClassBandhu Tuition is designed for Institutes and their authorised users. Students and parents/guardians do not currently have ordinary student-facing ClassBandhu Tuition logins; their information may be entered or managed by an Institute's authorised users. If you are a student or parent/guardian whose information an Institute has entered, please normally contact that Institute first about its use of the information. You may also contact us where we process the information on the Institute's behalf or where applicable law provides a direct route.

## 2. Who is responsible for the data

The responsible party depends on the purpose of processing.

**Institute-controlled records.** For student, parent/guardian, staff, admission, course, batch, attendance and tuition-fee records that an Institute chooses to enter for its own educational and administrative purposes, the Institute will generally decide why the information is used. The Institute will generally be responsible for notices, lawful purposes, permissions/consents and responding to individuals. ClassBandhu may process this Institute Data on the Institute's documented instructions to provide the Platform.

**ClassBandhu-controlled activities.** We may independently determine purposes for account creation and authentication, invitations, platform administration, subscription billing, security, support, public website enquiries and access requests. For those activities, we may be responsible for the relevant processing.

The actual legal role depends on the facts and law in force. The Institute Data Processing Agreement describes the intended processor arrangement for Institute Data; it does not automatically make ClassBandhu a processor for every activity.

## 3. Personal data we may handle

The data depends on the features used and information supplied. Categories may include:

1. **Account and authentication data:** name, email address, user/account identifiers, sign-in and invitation status, authentication and recovery information handled through the authentication provider, and role/membership records.
2. **Institute profile and configuration data:** Institute name, workspace address/slug, contact/profile details, settings and subscription administration details.
3. **Student and education data:** student name or identifier, admission/profile information, course and batch membership, attendance and reports, where those features are enabled and used.
4. **Parent/guardian and contact data:** names, phone numbers, email addresses and other contact details supplied by an Institute for its administration and communications.
5. **Financial and fee data:** course-fee obligations, discounts, installments, payments, payment references, balances, receipts, reversals and related history.
6. **Communication data:** reminder recipient details, message content prepared by an authorised user, billing contacts and communication preferences.
7. **Website enquiry data:** requester name, Institute name, email, optional phone and city, enquiry type, preferred contact method and optional message.
8. **Website access-request data:** requester name, Institute name, email, requested role, optional phone and city, message, request reference, status, review notes, linked invitation and administrative audit information.
9. **Security and operational data:** role and membership changes, invitation activity, audit events, diagnostics, service-health information and network/device information processed by the application or infrastructure providers. The website-intake gateway also derives an IP-based anti-abuse hash for rate-limiting and abuse prevention; infrastructure providers may independently process network metadata.
10. **Legal acceptance evidence:** user or Institute identifiers, document title, version, content hash, acceptance type and scope, timestamp, role and source needed to evidence the agreement or declaration.

We do not intend to collect information that is irrelevant to the stated purpose. Institutes should not upload identity documents, health details, biometric information or other high-risk data unless a feature expressly supports it and the Institute has separately assessed the need, lawful basis and safeguards.

## 4. Why information is used

Depending on the activity, information may be used to:
- create, authenticate and secure accounts;
- issue, administer and verify invitations;
- provide access to the correct Institute workspace and role;
- enable authorised Institute users to manage courses, batches, admissions, student profiles, fee records, payments, receipts, attendance and reports where enabled;
- calculate and display fee balances and maintain payment/receipt history;
- prepare user-initiated/manual WhatsApp reminder links;
- receive and respond to website enquiries and access requests;
- review, approve, reject or otherwise manage access requests;
- administer subscriptions, invoices and manual payment records;
- maintain tenant separation, role-based permissions and platform security;
- investigate suspected misuse, compromise or errors;
- provide support and respond to complaints or legal requests;
- evidence legal-document acceptance and declarations;
- comply with applicable law and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not describe student data as being used for targeted advertising. Any new analytics, profiling, AI feature or unrelated use involving personal data must be assessed, disclosed and supported by an appropriate legal basis before launch.

## 5. Children and student data

Institutes decide what student and parent/guardian information to enter. They must provide appropriate notices and obtain any consent, authorisation or other legal basis required by law. Where applicable law requires verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before processing a child's personal data, the responsible Data Fiduciary must comply and retain appropriate evidence.

An Institute Owner's onboarding declaration is not a substitute for parent or guardian consent and does not mean ClassBandhu has verified each Institute's consent records. The Institute Data Processing Agreement allocates the parties' intended responsibilities, subject to legal review.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 contains provisions concerning children's data and verifiable parental consent. Under the commencement notification, the substantive provisions including sections 3–17 and the corresponding operational Rules are scheduled for the eighteen-month commencement group calculated from 13 November 2025 (13 May 2027). The Act and Rules have staggered commencement, so the precise obligations applicable at a given time must be checked. Existing applicable law, including the IT Act and SPDI Rules where relevant, must also be considered during the transition. Educational-institution exemptions, if any, are not assumed to apply automatically to every Institute or to ClassBandhu.

The current Platform does not represent face recognition as an available attendance feature. Any future biometric or facial-recognition processing would require a separate necessity, privacy, security and legal assessment, appropriate notices and permissions, retention controls and an accessible alternative where appropriate.

## 6. WhatsApp and external communications

Current WhatsApp reminders are user-initiated/manual. The Platform may open WhatsApp or prepare a message for an authorised user; it does not currently promise automatic WhatsApp Business Cloud API delivery or automatic email/SMS delivery.

If an authorised user opens WhatsApp or sends a message, WhatsApp and its relevant providers process the information under their own terms and privacy practices. The sender must verify the recipient and content and respect applicable preferences and law. We do not control the external provider's independent processing.

## 9. Security

The Platform is designed with authenticated accounts, role-based access, Institute workspace separation, Supabase Auth, PostgreSQL, row-level security and database functions/RPCs. The website uses a server-side gateway for access requests and enquiries.

Those design elements do not prove that every control is error-free or that any certification exists. We do not claim 100% security, a certification, a penetration-test result, a particular encryption standard, backup frequency, recovery objective or uptime level unless independently verified and documented.

Users and Institutes must use strong unique passwords, protect devices and tokens, assign roles carefully, remove former staff promptly and report suspected compromise.

Platform Super Admin functions are intended for platform-level administration and not routine inspection of Institute academic records. Exceptional support or security access, if necessary, must be limited and handled under the applicable controls and DPA.

## 10. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide the service, administer accounts and subscriptions, maintain financial and legal records, protect security, investigate misuse, resolve disputes and comply with law.

Different categories require different periods. Exact retention periods for student records, attendance, payment and receipt records, website enquiries, access requests, invitation records, administrative notes, audit/security logs and backups have not yet been approved as a complete schedule. The Institute Data Processing Agreement must be read with the approved retention schedule.

When an Institute closes a workspace or requests export/deletion, we will review the Institute's authority and instructions, applicable law, financial-record requirements, legal holds, provider capabilities and backup limitations. A request does not mean all information can be erased immediately from every system or backup. Any retained data must be restricted to the purpose that justifies retention.

## 11. Data subject rights and privacy requests

Rights and procedures depend on the law in force and whether the Institute or ClassBandhu is responsible for the particular processing.

For Institute Data, a student, parent/guardian or staff member should normally contact the relevant Institute first. We will provide reasonable assistance where we process that information on the Institute's instructions.

For account, website enquiry, access-request, subscription or other ClassBandhu-controlled information, contact us at **info.classbandhu@gmail.com**. We may ask for proportionate information to verify identity and authority. Do not email passwords, OTPs, full payment-card details or unnecessary identity documents.

Where processing is based on consent and applicable law gives a right to withdraw it, the request can be sent through the relevant service or contact route. Withdrawal does not retrospectively make prior lawful processing unlawful and may affect the availability of a feature.
