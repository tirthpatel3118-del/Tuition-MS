## 1. Purpose and scope

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) applies to every person who accesses ClassBandhu Tuition, including Institute Owners, teachers, accountants, other authorised Institute users and Platform administrators.

ClassBandhu Tuition is an administrative platform for tuition centres, coaching institutes and similar educational organisations. Access is permitted only for legitimate, authorised educational and administrative purposes. This AUP does not cover a separate ClassBandhu School product unless a school-specific policy is approved.

## 2. Account and access security

Users must:
- use only their own account and never share passwords, one-time links, invitation links or authentication tokens;
- keep credentials, devices and recovery channels secure;
- use strong, unique passwords and available security controls;
- sign out from shared devices and protect devices against unauthorised access;
- promptly report suspected compromise, lost devices or unauthorised access;
- use only the roles and permissions assigned by the Institute.

Institute Owners must invite only authorised people, review user roles periodically and remove access promptly when staff leave or no longer require it.

## 3. Institute and tenant boundaries

Users must not:
- access, attempt to access, enumerate or infer another Institute's workspace or records;
- use another Institute's credentials, identifiers, invitations or exports;
- bypass or test tenant isolation, role checks, row-level security, authentication or administrative controls without written authorisation;
- share Institute Data with another Institute or an unauthorised third party;
- use Super Admin or support privileges to inspect Institute content for curiosity or unrelated purposes;
- attempt to use an invitation issued to another email address or person.

Any suspected cross-Institute exposure or access-control weakness must be reported promptly.

## 4. Student, child and parent/guardian data

Users must:
- enter only information reasonably necessary for the Institute's educational or administrative purpose;
- provide required privacy notices and obtain legally required permission or consent before entering or using information;
- maintain evidence of parent/lawful-guardian consent where applicable;
- avoid collecting identity, health, biometric or other high-risk information unless the feature, purpose and legal basis have been separately assessed and approved;
- keep student contact details, addresses, fee information, attendance and reports confidential;
- share or export student records only with people who have a legitimate need and authority;
- correct inaccurate or outdated information;
- respond appropriately to student, parent/guardian and staff privacy requests.

An Institute Owner's click-through declaration is not a substitute for any separate parent or guardian consent required by law.

## 5. Financial and attendance integrity

Users must not falsify, manipulate, conceal or improperly alter:
- student fee balances, discounts, installments, payments, allocations, reversals or receipts;
- subscription invoices or payment confirmation;
- attendance dates, statuses, corrections or audit history;
- admissions, student details, Institute identity or authorisation information.

Users must not use another person's payment confirmation or receipt. Financial or attendance discrepancies should be reported to the Institute's authorised administrator and, where appropriate, ClassBandhu support.

## 6. Messaging and communications

Current WhatsApp reminders are manual/user-initiated. Users must:
- verify the recipient and message before sending;
- send only lawful, relevant and appropriate communications;
- respect opt-outs and communication preferences where applicable;
- avoid spam, threats, harassment, coercion or disclosure of another student's information;
- not imply that a manual WhatsApp link means ClassBandhu automatically delivered, monitored or verified the message.

Automated WhatsApp Business API, email or SMS delivery is not represented as a current feature.

## 7. Prohibited content and conduct

Users must not use the Platform to:
- commit or facilitate unlawful activity, fraud, identity theft or financial deception;
- harass, threaten, discriminate against, exploit or abuse any person;
- upload malware, malicious scripts or harmful files;
- distribute spam, phishing, scams or unsolicited bulk messages;
- upload content that infringes intellectual-property, privacy or publicity rights;
- conduct unauthorised surveillance or profiling;
- expose children's personal information publicly or unnecessarily;
- upload data obtained unlawfully or without authority;
- impersonate another person, Institute or official;
- interfere with the availability, integrity or security of the Platform.

## 8. Technical restrictions and security testing

Except where expressly authorised in writing or permitted by applicable law, users must not:
- probe, scan, penetrate, stress-test or exploit the Platform;
- reverse engineer, decompile or attempt to derive source code, except to the extent such restriction is prohibited by law;
- scrape, harvest or bulk-extract data;
- circumvent rate limits, access controls or security safeguards;
- introduce bots, scripts or automation that degrade service or access data beyond the user's permissions;
- test Production systems using real personal data without written approval.

Security researchers must contact **info.classbandhu@gmail.com** and obtain written scope and permission before testing. Do not include passwords, authentication tokens or unnecessary student records in a first report.

## 9. Exports, devices and local copies

Exports may contain student, parent/guardian and financial information. Users must:
- export only what is needed for an authorised purpose;
- store exports on access-controlled devices and approved locations;
- avoid public links, personal social accounts and unsecured shared folders;
- delete unnecessary local copies securely;
- not transfer records to personal accounts or third parties without authority;
- promptly report lost or exposed exports.

## 10. Reporting a violation or security concern

Report suspected misuse, vulnerabilities, accidental disclosure, account compromise or policy violations to **info.classbandhu@gmail.com**.

Include the affected Institute/workspace, approximate time, affected feature and a concise description. Do not send passwords, OTPs or unnecessary student records in the initial report.

## 11. Enforcement

Depending on severity and applicable law, the Operator may investigate, preserve relevant evidence, restrict a session, suspend an account or workspace, disable an invitation, require access changes or take other proportionate steps to protect users and the Platform.

Where practicable, the affected Institute will be notified and given an opportunity to respond. Immediate action may be necessary to prevent harm, address compromise or comply with law.

Subscription suspension and any grace period are governed by the approved Terms and commercial schedule. Suspension does not by itself mean Institute Data is deleted.

## 12. Changes

This AUP may be updated as the Platform and legal requirements evolve. Material changes will be versioned, and users will be asked to accept the current required version where appropriate.
