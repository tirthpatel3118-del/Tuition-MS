# ClassBandhu — Acceptable Use Policy

**Prepared:** 25 November 2026; revised 26 November 2026 (v0.4)  
**Status:** NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION  
**Operator:** Tirth Patel  
**Contact:** info.classbandhu@gmail.com  
**Applies to:** ClassBandhu Tuition. ClassBandhu School is a separate product and is not covered until a school-specific policy is approved.

## 1. Purpose and scope
This Acceptable Use Policy (**AUP**) applies to every person who accesses ClassBandhu, including Institute Owners, teachers, accountants, other authorised Institute users and platform administrators. It supplements the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. If a stricter legal or security requirement applies, that requirement controls.

ClassBandhu is an administrative platform for tuition/institute workflows. Access is limited to legitimate, authorised educational and administrative purposes.

## 2. Account and access security
Users must:
- use only their own account and never share passwords or authentication tokens;
- keep credentials, devices and recovery channels secure;
- use strong unique passwords and any additional security controls made available;
- sign out from shared devices and protect devices against unauthorised access;
- promptly report suspected account compromise, lost devices or unauthorised access;
- ensure that each staff member has their own account where supported;
- use only the roles and permissions assigned by the Institute.

Institute Owners must review users and permissions periodically and remove access promptly when a staff member leaves or changes responsibilities.

## 3. Institute and tenant boundaries
Users must not:
- access, attempt to access, enumerate or infer another Institute's workspace or records;
- use another Institute's credentials, identifiers, invitations or exports;
- bypass or test tenant isolation, role checks, RLS, authentication or administrative controls without written authorisation;
- share Institute Data with another Institute or an unauthorised third party;
- use Super Admin or support privileges to inspect Institute content for curiosity or unrelated purposes.

Any suspected cross-Institute exposure must be reported immediately.

## 4. Student, child and parent/guardian data
Users must:
- enter only information reasonably necessary for the Institute's educational/administrative purpose;
- provide required privacy notices and obtain legally required authority/consent before entering data;
- maintain evidence of parent/lawful-guardian consent where applicable;
- avoid collecting sensitive identity, health, biometric or other high-risk data unless the feature, purpose and legal basis have been separately assessed and approved;
- keep student contact details, addresses, fee information, attendance and reports confidential;
- share/export student records only with people who have a legitimate need and authority;
- correct inaccurate or outdated information;
- respond appropriately to student, parent/guardian and staff privacy requests.

The Institute Owner's click-through declaration is not a substitute for each parent's or guardian's consent where required.

## 5. Financial and attendance integrity
Users must not falsify or manipulate:
- student fee balances, payments, allocations, reversals or receipts;
- subscription invoices or payment confirmation;
- attendance dates, statuses, correction requests or audit history;
- admission, student or Institute identity details.

Users must not use another person's payment confirmation or receipt. Financial discrepancies should be reported through the Institute's authorised administrator and support route.

## 6. Messaging and communications
Current WhatsApp reminder features are manual. Users are responsible for checking the recipient and message content before sending. Users must:
- send only appropriate, lawful and relevant communications;
- respect opt-outs and communication preferences where applicable;
- avoid spam, threats, harassment, coercion or disclosure of another student's information;
- verify that the phone number belongs to the intended recipient;
- not imply that a manual WhatsApp link means ClassBandhu automatically delivered or verified the message.

Automated WhatsApp Business API, email or SMS delivery is not promised as a current feature.

## 7. Prohibited content and conduct
Users must not use ClassBandhu to:
- commit or facilitate unlawful activity, fraud, identity theft or financial deception;
- harass, threaten, discriminate against, exploit or abuse any person;
- upload malware, malicious scripts or harmful files;
- distribute spam, phishing, scams or unsolicited bulk messages;
- upload content that infringes intellectual-property, privacy or publicity rights;
- conduct unauthorised surveillance or profiling;
- expose children's personal information publicly or unnecessarily;
- upload data obtained unlawfully or without authority;
- impersonate another person, Institute or official;
- interfere with availability, integrity or security of the Platform.

## 8. Technical restrictions
Except where expressly authorised in writing or permitted by applicable law, users must not:
- probe, scan, penetrate, stress-test or exploit the Platform;
- reverse engineer, decompile or attempt to derive source code;
- scrape, harvest or bulk-extract data;
- circumvent rate limits, access controls or security safeguards;
- introduce code, bots or automation that degrades service or accesses data beyond the user's permissions;
- test production systems using real personal data without approval.

Security researchers should contact info.classbandhu@gmail.com before conducting testing and must obtain written scope and permission.

## 9. Data exports, devices and local copies
Exports may contain sensitive student and financial information. Users must:
- export only what is needed;
- store exports on access-controlled devices and approved locations;
- avoid public links, personal social accounts or shared unsecured folders;
- delete unnecessary local copies securely;
- not transfer records to personal accounts or third parties without authority;
- promptly report lost or exposed exports.

## 10. Reporting a violation
Report suspected misuse, security vulnerabilities, accidental disclosure or policy violations to **info.classbandhu@gmail.com**. Include the relevant Institute/workspace, approximate time, affected feature and a concise description. Do not send passwords or unnecessary student records in the initial report.

## 11. Enforcement
Depending on severity and applicable law, the Operator may investigate, preserve relevant evidence, restrict a session, suspend an account or workspace, disable an invitation, or take other proportionate steps to protect users and the Platform. Where practicable, the affected Institute will be notified and given an opportunity to respond. Immediate action may be necessary to prevent harm, address compromise or comply with law.

Suspension for non-payment follows the separate Terms and the agreed seven-day grace period. Suspension does not itself mean that Institute Data is deleted.

## 12. Changes
This AUP may be updated as the Platform and legal requirements evolve. Material changes will be versioned and users will be asked to accept the current required version where appropriate.
