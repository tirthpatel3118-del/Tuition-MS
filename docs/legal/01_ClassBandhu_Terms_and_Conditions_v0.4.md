6.5 **Seven-day grace period (proposed operational rule):** The due date is day zero. The Institute has seven (7) full calendar days after the due date to make payment. Unless the invoice expressly states a different approved timezone, the deadline is 23:59:59 India Standard Time (IST; Asia/Kolkata) on the seventh calendar day after the due date. Weekends and public holidays count as calendar days. The timezone and exact billing configuration must be confirmed before this clause is activated in the product.

6.6 A successful payment made by the deadline will protect the Institute from non-payment suspension while the Operator completes reasonable verification, provided the Institute submits the transaction evidence described below by the deadline. Verification delay alone must not cause suspension. For this clause, **transaction evidence** means the payment-provider, bank or payment-channel generated transaction reference (such as a UTR, UPI transaction ID, RRN or equivalent bank/payment reference), the amount paid, the transaction date (and time where available), the payment channel, the intended recipient/payee, and a transaction status or record reasonably showing that the transfer was successful or completed. Where a payment channel does not issue a UTR, UPI ID or RRN, an equivalent provider-generated reference or relevant bank-statement entry may be supplied. A screenshot may support the evidence but, by itself, is not sufficient unless it displays verifiable provider-generated transaction details. The Institute must not send passwords, PINs, OTPs, full card details or other authentication secrets. If the payment is later shown to have failed, been reversed or not reached the designated destination, the Operator will notify the Institute, provide the verification basis where reasonably possible, and allow five (5) calendar days to resolve or replace the payment before non-payment suspension, unless urgent fraud/security action is justified.

6.7 A partial payment does not automatically satisfy the invoice. Unless a written payment arrangement says otherwise, the unpaid balance remains due. Before suspending for a partial payment, the Operator must identify the remaining amount and give notice. A bona fide invoice dispute must be submitted in writing before the grace-period deadline, identify the specific invoice item and amount disputed, explain the grounds with reasonable supporting material, and confirm payment of the undisputed portion by the applicable due date or grace-period deadline. A dispute that does not meet these conditions does not pause suspension for the unpaid amount. The Operator will acknowledge a qualifying dispute within two (2) business days and complete an initial review within five (5) business days after receiving the required information. If reasonably necessary, the Operator may extend the review once by up to five (5) further business days, notifying the Institute before the initial review period ends and giving a brief reason. For this clause, a **business day** means a day other than a Sunday or a public holiday on which banks are generally open for business in India. The disputed amount alone will not be a basis for non-payment suspension while this time-limited review is pending, subject to fraud/security exceptions and applicable law. If the dispute is rejected in whole or in part, the Operator will explain the decision and identify the amount payable; the Institute must pay that amount within five (5) calendar days after the decision notice, failing which suspension may proceed after notice. If the Operator has not issued a decision by the end of the maximum ten (10) business-day review period, the Operator must send a status notice; the disputed amount may then be treated as unpaid for suspension purposes only after a further five (5) calendar days' written notice. This does not determine the merits of the dispute, waive either party's legal rights, or prevent the Institute from pursuing the dispute through the contract's dispute-resolution route. The review pause therefore has a defined maximum and does not create an indefinite payment holiday.

Reminder timing/channel is an operational target, not a guarantee until implemented and tested.
